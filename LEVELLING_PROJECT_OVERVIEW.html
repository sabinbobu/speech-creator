Automatic static levelling on ILCU, driven by an IMU

How the ported DPE levelling chain is meant to work on the Al077a board, what each part does, and what happens in the situations a demo will hit. State of 2026-11-11. Evidence and patches are in LEVELLING_PORT_REPORT.md.

The idea in four sentences

When the rear of a car is loaded, the front rises and the low beam points too high. The IMU measures how far the body is pitched front-up. AvacStat1 turns that angle into a lamp angle that lowers the beam by the same amount, and only after the change has stayed for a moment. CddDcm then sets an analog voltage on the LWR_DC pin; the levelling actuator in the headlamp moves the reflector to the position that voltage stands for.

Three DPE modules were ported unchanged in their logic: AvacStat1 (how much), CtrlDcm (whether, and to what limits), CddDcm (how, electrically). One new file, ILCU_LevellingAdapter.c, connects them to ILCU: it supplies the pitch, the supply and feedback voltages, and the outputs.

Where things stand

works on host swc_levelling

Pitch input, signal quality, supply and feedback reading, safe start and demo timing are in place. Eight scenarios pass in a host harness that runs the real chain against a model of the real output stage.

blocks the demo three things outside it

B1 Rte.c does not call the chain. B2 swc_imu drives the LWR pins itself. B3 the IARA pitch stays 0 because swc_imu takes every IMU sample first.

not proven target build and board

Nothing was built with Keil or run on the ECU. Pitch sign, the TI driver's behaviour after PWM idle, and the supply divider values need a bench check.

The output stage on the board

The levelling actuator has its own position control. The ECU does not drive a motor winding and never sees the lamp position. It sets a voltage between 20 % and 80 % of the supply and holds it for a calculated time; the actuator moves to the position that ratio stands for. What the ADC reads back is that command voltage, used to keep the ratio exact and to detect a short circuit.

The three state machines

Working scenarios

Each scenario lists what you should see, with the debugger variable or the pin to look at. Voltages are for a 12.0 V supply. Scenarios 1 to 7 were run in the host harness; scenario 8 is a procedure.

4 · Tilt nose-down

Nothing moves, by design. Static levelling only lowers the beam; a car that dips at the front already points its beam down. AvacStat1 clamps a negative car angle to 0, so the lamp stays at 0° and LWR_DC stays at 2.40 V during a move. For a demo, always tilt front-up.

6 · Supply voltage leaves 8.6 to 17.7 V

CddDcm switches the output off and locks. When the supply returns it re-drives the last target. Because the output is a ratio of the supply, the same lamp angle gives 7.20 V at 12 V and 9.01 V at 15 V.

7 · LWR_DC shorted to ground

During a move the read-back stays below 6 % of the supply. After 400 ms of a 500 ms window CddDcm reports DCMotor_SC_GND and switches off; 2 s later CtrlDcm goes to Fail. Look at ILCU_LvlAdp_DemStatus[23] = 3.

8 · Bench test without the IMU

Proves the chain and the hardware path before the pitch source works. In the debugger set ILCU_LvlAdp_BenchPitch_0p1mrad to a value and ILCU_LvlAdp_BenchPitchEnable to 1.
